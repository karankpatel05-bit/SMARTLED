1. Wi-Fi Setup

Plug in your SmartLED. If it is new or just reset, open your phone's Wi-Fi settings and connect to the network starting with SMARTLED_SETUP_ (e.g., SMARTLED_SETUP_A1B2...) to bridge it to your home Wi-Fi.

2. Power & Discovery

Once connected to your home network, the SmartLED will continuously broadcast its signal to this app as long as it is powered on.

3. Add Your Device

Check the 'Devices' tab here in Settings. Your new device will appear under Discovered Devices. Tap 'Add' to name it!

4. Manual Control

Go back to the main dashboard. Use the slider to smoothly adjust brightness, or tap the power icon to toggle the light.

5. AI Gestures & Voice

Tap the blue target icon on any device card. Now, speak voice commands, or show 5 fingers to the camera for max brightness, and a closed fist to turn it off!
